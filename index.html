Blackjack Rules

This is a quick player-versus-player version. There is no dealer: everyone draws from one shuffled 52-card deck and competes for the best hand.

Anyone in the lobby can enter and join the table. The first player seated is the table lead and can deal each hand; only the lobby host can add the test bot.

  • Everyone starts with two cards.
  • 2–10 are worth their number; J, Q and K are worth 10.
  • An Ace counts as 11 when that keeps you at 21 or below; otherwise it counts as 1.
  • Choose Hit to draw one card or Stand to keep your total. At 21 you stand automatically.
  • Going over 21 is a bust and cannot win.
  • The closest total to 21 wins. A two-card 21 (Ace plus a 10-value card) beats any other 21. Tied best hands share the win; if everyone busts, nobody wins.

Other players' cards stay hidden until the hand ends. The table lead can end a hand early. This quick-play version has no splits, doubles, insurance, or house dealer.
